Why Install Biogas?

💸

Slash Fuel Bills

No more buying charcoal, firewood or LPG. Cook every day for free using your own cow dung and kitchen waste.

🌾

Free Fertilizer

Bio-slurry is a superior organic fertilizer that increases harvests and saves on expensive chemical fertilizers.

🌳

Save Trees

One household biogas plant saves ~5 tonnes of firewood per year — protecting our forests for future generations.

🧹

Cleaner & Safer

No smoke, no soot, no charcoal dust. Better health for your family — especially women and children.

🦟

Less Disease

Digesting waste kills pathogens and flies, dramatically reducing household illness and odour.

📈

Quick Payback

Most systems pay for themselves in 12–24 months through fuel and fertilizer savings alone.
